## What this does not cover

**Podcast transcripts and the text of essays.** They are the copyrighted work
of their publishers and authors and are deliberately **not** included in this
repository. `.gitignore` excludes them, and `python run.py fetch <source>`
regenerates them locally from the publisher's own site for personal use.

**The underlying episodes and posts**, which belong to their publishers,
authors and hosts.

The notes are original summaries and analysis written from those sources. They
paraphrase rather than reproduce, quoting no more than a short phrase where a
speaker's or author's exact wording matters. Every note links to its source.
