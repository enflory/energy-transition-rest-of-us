## What this does not cover

**Podcast transcripts.** They are the copyrighted work of their publishers and
are deliberately **not** included in this repository. `.gitignore` excludes
them, and `python run.py fetch <show>` regenerates them locally from the
publisher's own site for personal use.

**The underlying episodes**, which belong to their publishers and hosts.

The notes are original summaries and analysis written from those episodes. They
paraphrase rather than reproduce, quoting no more than a short phrase where a
speaker's exact wording matters. Every note links to the source episode.
